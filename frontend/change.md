# Engineering Deterministic Decisions: A Blueprint for System Prompts in an Indian Market LLM Trading System

## Foundational Principles for Prompt Engineering in Financial Agentic Systems

The design of a multi-agent Large Language Model (LLM) trading system for the Indian equity markets (National Stock Exchange / Bombay Stock Exchange) necessitates a rigorous approach to prompt engineering that transcends simple instruction-giving. It requires the construction of a deterministic decision pipeline where each component's output is predictable, verifiable, and grounded in specific data [[86](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.19337v1)]. The core principles guiding this process are the prevention of hallucination through absolute data grounding, the enforcement of structural integrity via strict schema validation, and the contextualization of every agent's reasoning within the unique regulatory and economic landscape of India. These principles are not merely technical preferences; they are foundational to mitigating the significant risks associated with deploying agentic AI in high-stakes financial environments, where model errors can lead to severe consequences including financial loss and data leakage [[9](https://openreview.net/pdf?id=kabNlhXQkT)]. The failure of many AI agents in production is rarely due to the underlying models themselves, but rather to the systems and processes built around them, highlighting the critical importance of robust prompt design as a form of context engineering [[46](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/agus-sudjianto-76519619_scalable-runtime-governance-for-agentic-ai-activity-7449413273940209664-cSfr), [119](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/ram-vegiraju-81272b162_the-anatomy-of-an-ai-agent-in-2026-activity-7454646807877488640-X2vw)].

The foremost principle is the elimination of hallucination, which involves compelling the LLM to synthesize information from provided sources rather than generating content from its general-world knowledge. This is achieved through several interconnected techniques. First, **persona anchoring** establishes a clear expert identity for each agent, constraining its reasoning within a specific domain, such as "Technical Analyst" or "Value Investor" [[6](https://www.mdpi.com/2078-2489/17/1/54)]. This creates a predictable framework for the agent's responses. Second, and most critically, is the mandate for **explicit data citation**. Every claim, assertion, or conclusion generated by an agent must be directly tied to a specific data point from its input payload. For instance, a researcher cannot state a price target based on intuition; it must cite the exact `valuation_score` from the Fundamentals Analyst or the `key_support` level from the Market Analyst that informs its calculation. If a required piece of evidence is absent, the agent must state "Insufficient evidence in upstream data" rather than fabricating a justification. This transforms the LLM into a sophisticated data processor and synthesizer, not a generative storyteller [[161](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/dan-hunter-ab8803131_logic-programming-slides-activity-7368574265807278080-b_KB)]. Third, **structured input/output enforcement** using schemas like Pydantic is non-negotiable. This ensures that downstream agents receive consistent, machine-readable data, preventing cascading failures that could derail the entire trading workflow [[50](https://arxiv.org/html/2509.13471v2), [200](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/mohith-gowda-k_ai-artificialintelligence-generativeai-activity-7426598264134656000-6xNB)]. The system should be designed to automatically retry an agent's generation if its output fails schema validation, creating a resilient feedback loop [[141](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/vigneshbalasubramaniam_are-you-still-worried-about-llm-outputs-not-activity-7395443182278410240-FC5C)].

The second pillar is the creation of a structurally sound and deterministic system. In finance, backends must remain deterministic even when interacting with probabilistic engines like LLMs [[72](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/kwkramer_im-obsessed-right-now-with-figuring-out-activity-7461091032735072256-PcnB)]. This is enforced by requiring all agents that produce JSON to do so without any conversational or explanatory text—only the pure JSON object is acceptable. This prevents ambiguity and ensures seamless integration between the specialized agents. The use of constrained decoding, where the LLM's output is forced to conform to a predefined JSON schema at the sampling stage, is a powerful technique for achieving this reliability [[46](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/agus-sudjianto-76519619_scalable-runtime-governance-for-agentic-ai-activity-7449413273940209664-cSfr)]. Furthermore, the system's governance structure, particularly the veto mechanism of the Conservative Risk Officer, must be deterministic. This means its rules must be unambiguous and executable, moving beyond qualitative judgment to include quantitative triggers. For example, the prompt for the Conservative Risk Officer should embed a rule such as, "If the probability of structural risk exceeds 0.8 and the confidence score is above 0.8, you MUST issue a 'BEARISH' verdict." This makes the veto a pre-execution control, not a post-facto observation, ensuring capital preservation is prioritized above all else [[121](https://dev.to/yuer/authority-boundaries-and-final-veto-in-ai-systems-18d9)].

Finally, the entire system must be deeply contextualized for the Indian market. Generic financial prompts will fail because they lack awareness of local regulations, economic indicators, and market dynamics. The prompts for each agent must be infused with this specific knowledge. This includes awareness of SEBI's new algo trading framework, which mandates broker accountability, order traceability via Algo IDs, and restrictions on API usage [[11](https://cskruti.com/sebis-2025-algo-trading-framework-a-practical-guide/), [23](https://www.quantinsti.com/articles/algorithmic-trading-india/)]. Agents like the TRADER and PORTFOLIO MANAGER must incorporate compliance checks related to these rules. The analysts must be primed to interpret data through an Indian lens. For example, the FUNDAMENTALS ANALYST should compare a company's P/E ratio against the Nifty 50 average (~22.8) and relevant sectoral indices, considering how recent inflation data (CPI/WPI) impacts rate-sensitive sectors [[32](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/nilesh-sharma-688bb19a_nifty-stockmarketindia-valuation-activity-7352974390646423552-A6Vf), [33](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/acs-global-5a4021169_beyond-the-numbers-mapping-niftys-future-activity-7385535180595343360-h2hM), [113](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/madhavankutty-g-57b5295_privatecaoex-manufacturing-wpi-activity-7319761065318199299-s3MC)]. The SOCIAL MEDIA and NEWS ANALYSTS must be trained to detect signals unique to India, such as political instability in neighboring regions, the impact of Initial Public Offerings (IPOs) on rival firms, or shifts in Foreign Institutional Investor (FII) and Domestic Institutional Investor (DII) flows, which are known to significantly influence Indian markets [[25](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2590291123002486), [34](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/moneycontrol_marketswithmc-dii-fii-activity-7444427761747767297-ZJKP), [66](https://www.scribd.com/document/969014361/Free-Press-Journal-20-12-2025)]. By embedding these principles—data grounding, structural determinism, and contextual specificity—the system can function as a cohesive, reliable, and explainable expert system, capable of navigating the complexities of the Indian equity markets [[86](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.19337v1)].

## Agent-Specific Prompt Designs: Analysts and Researchers

The foundation of the trading system rests upon the quality of analysis produced by the initial set of agents: Market, Social Media, News, and Fundamentals Analysts. Their prompts must be engineered to produce precise, data-grounded insights that serve as the bedrock for subsequent decision-making. Each prompt leverages persona anchoring to define a specific expert role and enforces strict adherence to the provided input data, ensuring that all outputs are factual summaries rather than speculative interpretations.

**1. MARKET ANALYST**
This agent's sole responsibility is to distill complex technical data into a structured summary. Its prompt anchors it as a "Technical Analyst, Level 1," a persona implying expertise in standard charting and indicator interpretation without overreaching into predictive modeling.
*   **Full System Prompt Text:**
    You are a Technical Analyst, Level 1. Your task is to analyze the provided OHLCV data and technical indicators. Do not make predictions or give investment advice. Your output must be a strict, Pydantic-validated JSON object summarizing the current market condition based *exclusively* on the input data.
    - `current_trend`: Based on Moving Averages and price action relative to key levels. Use 'uptrend', 'downtrend', or 'sideways'.
    - `key_support`: A list of float values representing the strongest support levels identified from the OHLCV data.
    - `key_resistance`: A list of float values representing the strongest resistance levels identified from the OHLCV data.
    - `momentum_state`: A string describing momentum based on RSI and MACD crossovers. Use 'strong_bullish', 'weak_bullish', 'neutral', 'weak_bearish', or 'strong_bearish'.
    - `institutional_flow_bias`: A string indicating bias based on volume spikes. Use 'inflow', 'outflow', or 'neutral'. Cite the specific volume value from the input that supports this.
    - `technical_verdict`: A single word verdict: 'BULLISH', 'BEARISH', or 'NEUTRAL'. This should be a synthesis of `current_trend`, `momentum_state`, and `institutional_flow_bias`. Every statement must be grounded in the input data.
    Output ONLY the JSON object, no other text.
*   **Rationale and Techniques:**
    The prompt's effectiveness stems from its strict role definition ("Technical Analyst, Level 1") and its prohibition of prediction, which prevents the LLM from hallucinating future prices. The techniques employed are:
    *   **Persona Anchoring:** Defining the agent's role as a junior analyst focused on description, not prediction, sets clear behavioral boundaries [[6](https://www.mdpi.com/2078-2489/17/1/54)].
    *   **Structured Output Enforcement:** The explicit instruction to output a "strict, Pydantic-validated JSON object" and the detailed schema for each field enforce the necessary format for downstream agents [[46](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/agus-sudjianto-76519619_scalable-runtime-governance-for-agentic-ai-activity-7449413273940209664-cSfr), [107](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/hjalli_llms-will-change-the-way-we-work-in-such-activity-7379851587311038464-EsTv)].
    *   **Chain-of-Thought Reasoning:** The prompt guides the agent through a logical process: identify trend from MAs, find support/resistance from price action, assess momentum from RSI/MACD, and infer flow bias from volume. This structured reasoning leads to more coherent outputs [[124](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/swaroopkallakuri_a-reddit-user-spent-1000-hours-testing-prompts-activity-7420103175363375104-JdBV)].
    *   **Strict Data Grounding:** The repeated emphasis on basing conclusions "exclusively on the input data" and the specific instruction to "cite the specific volume value" are crucial for preventing fabrication. The agent is treated as a data summarizer, not an oracle [[161](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/dan-hunter-ab8803131_logic-programming-slides-activity-7368574265807278080-b_KB)].

**2. SOCIAL MEDIA ANALYST**
This agent analyzes social sentiment and identifies divergences with price action, a critical signal for potential reversals. The persona of a "Social Sentiment Specialist" focuses its analytical lens on public discourse.
*   **Full System Prompt Text:**
    You are a Social Sentiment Specialist analyzing aggregated social media data for a stock ticker. Your task is to quantify sentiment and identify contradictions with the asset's price movement.
    - `aggregate_sentiment`: A float score from -1.0 (extremely negative) to +1.0 (extremely positive), based on the provided social data.
    - `is_high_engagement`: A boolean. True if total engagement (likes, shares, comments) is in the top 20% of historical norms for this ticker.
    - `sentiment_divergence`: A boolean. True if `aggregate_sentiment` contradicts the `current_trend` from the Market Analyst. False otherwise.
    - `divergence_rationale`: A string explaining the divergence. Example: "Aggregate social sentiment is '+0.65' while the price is in a confirmed 'downtrend'. This suggests retail optimism is out of sync with institutional selling pressure."
    - `social_verdict`: A string verdict: 'DIVERGENCE_SIGNAL', 'CONFIRMING_SIGNAL', or 'NO_SIGNAL'. Base this on `sentiment_divergence`.
    Output ONLY the JSON object, no other text.
*   **Rationale and Techniques:**
    The prompt is designed to create a clear, actionable signal for the portfolio manager. Key techniques include:
    *   **Data Grounding:** The agent must derive its `aggregate_sentiment` and `is_high_engagement` directly from the provided social data payload, ensuring its analysis is tethered to the source material.
    *   **Cross-Agent Integration:** The requirement to check `sentiment_divergence` against the `current_trend` from the Market Analyst forces the agent to perform a cross-referential analysis, a core component of its value-add [[76](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/parikshit-tandon-5b688112a_promptengineering-ai-stockmarket-activity-7383386235320463360-0XkP)].
    *   **Structured Verdict:** The `social_verdict` provides a clear, pre-defined outcome that simplifies interpretation for the next stage of the workflow. The inclusion of a rationale ensures transparency.

**3. NEWS ANALYST**
This agent acts as a filter and interpreter for global news, identifying material events relevant to the specific Indian stock being analyzed. The persona of a "Global Macro News Crawler" positions it to scan a wide range of sources for systemic risks and opportunities.
*   **Full System Prompt Text:*
    You are a Global Macro News Crawler. Your task is to analyze the provided global news headlines and articles related to the specified Indian stock ticker.
    - `sentiment_score`: A float from -1.0 to +1.0, indicating the overall sentiment of the news items towards the company/sector.
    - `catalyst_identified`: A boolean. True if any news item represents a significant catalyst (e.g., major regulatory change, key contract win, geopolitical event affecting operations).
    - `primary_driver`: A string describing the main theme driving the sentiment (e.g., "US-China Trade Policy," "RBI Interest Rate Decision," "New Environmental Regulation").
    - `driver_source`: A string containing the URL or source identifier of the article that best exemplifies the `primary_driver`.
    - `news_verdict`: A string verdict: 'BEARISH_CATALYST', 'BULLISH_CATALYST', or 'NO_MATERIAL_IMPACT'. Justify your verdict based on the potential impact on the Indian market.
    Output ONLY the JSON object, no other text.
*   **Rationale and Techniques:**
    The prompt ensures the agent provides concise, evidence-based intelligence. The key techniques are:
    *   **Source Citation:** The `driver_source` field forces the agent to anchor its reasoning to a specific, verifiable piece of information, which is critical for auditability and trust [[169](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/tdelistathis_%F0%9D%97%9C-%F0%9D%97%9B%F0%9D%97%94%F0%9D%97%A9%F0%9D%97%98%F0%9D%97%A1%F0%9D%97%A7-%F0%9D%97%AA%F0%9D%97%A5%F0%9D%97%9C%F0%9D%97%A7%F0%9D%97%A7%F0%9D%97%98%F0%9D%97%A1-%F0%9D%97%96%F0%9D%97%A2%F0%9D%97%97%F0%9D%97%98-activity-7439158758842236928-JYVB), [184](https://www.support.marketplace.spglobal.com/content/dam/spglobal/mi/en/documents/marketplace/newsletters/2025/marketplace_data__solutions_communique_january_31_2025.pdf)].
    *   **Impact-Oriented Analysis:** The focus is not just on sentiment but on whether the news constitutes a "catalyst" and what the "primary driver" is. This moves the analysis from descriptive to prescriptive.
    *   **Contextual Relevance:** The instruction to justify the verdict based on potential impact "on the Indian market" directs the agent's focus to locally relevant consequences, filtering out purely international noise.

**4. FUNDAMENTALS ANALYST**
This agent performs a deep dive into a company's financial statements, providing a valuation and health assessment. Given the user's directive to prioritize fundamentals, this agent's prompt is the most detailed. The persona of a "Value Investor" aligns its objective with long-term intrinsic value.
*   **Full System Prompt Text:**
    You are a Value Investor analyzing the provided financial statements (Balance Sheet, Cash Flow, Income Statement) and ratios (PE, ROE, Debt Ratios). Your analysis must prioritize long-term fundamental strength over short-term noise.
    - `valuation_score`: An integer from 1-10. Score based on PE vs. sector average, ROE stability, and debt-to-equity ratio. Cite the specific metric values used for scoring.
    - `health_status`: A string verdict: 'STRONG_HEALTH', 'MODERATE_RISK', or 'WEAK_HEALTH'. Base this on debt levels, profitability trends, and cash flow adequacy.
    - `primary_strength`: A string identifying the single greatest fundamental strength observed (e.g., "High and stable ROE," "Strong free cash flow generation," "Low debt-to-equity ratio").
    - `primary_weakness`: A string identifying the single greatest fundamental weakness observed (e.g., "Declining profit margins," "High reliance on short-term debt," "Negative operating cash flow").
    - `overall_fundamental_verdict`: A string verdict: 'BUY_SIGNAL', 'CAUTION', or 'SELL_SIGNAL'. This should be a synthesis of `valuation_score` and `health_status`.
    Output ONLY the JSON object, no other text.
*   **Rationale and Techniques:**
    This prompt is designed to produce a comprehensive, data-driven fundamental assessment.
    *   **Comparative Analysis:** The instruction to score based on "PE vs. sector average" explicitly incorporates comparative analysis, a cornerstone of fundamental valuation [[32](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/nilesh-sharma-688bb19a_nifty-stockmarketindia-valuation-activity-7352974390646423552-A6Vf), [33](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/acs-global-5a4021169_beyond-the-numbers-mapping-niftys-future-activity-7385535180595343360-h2hM)].
    *   **Strength/Weakness Focus:** By asking for the "single greatest" strength and weakness, the prompt forces the agent to synthesize a large amount of data into a concise, memorable insight.
    *   **Integrated Verdict:** The `overall_fundamental_verdict` synthesizes the `valuation_score` and `health_status` into a final, actionable signal, completing the analyst's report.

## Agent-Specific Prompt Designs: Risk Officers and Oracles

The agents in this category—Aggressive Risk Officer, Conservative Risk Officer, and Neutral Arbitrator—are tasked with evaluating the trade proposals generated by the researchers. Their prompts are designed to instill different risk philosophies, culminating in a robust vetting process. The most critical design challenge here is creating a deterministic veto mechanism that guarantees capital preservation.

**5. BULL RESEARCHER**
This agent is tasked with building the most optimistic case possible for a trade, acting as a devil's advocate for the buy side. Its persona is that of a "ruthless Permabull."
*   **Full System Prompt Text:**
    You are a ruthless Permabull. Your job is to construct the most extreme, high-upside thesis possible for a trade. Ignore any bearish data unless it mathematically invalidates the possibility of a +20% upside move.
    - `bias`: Must be 'PERMABULL'. Do not deviate.
    - `thesis_summary`: A concise paragraph building the case for a significant long position. Weave together bullish signals from all four analysts.
    - `primary_catalyst`: A string identifying the single most important catalyst supporting your thesis.
    - `supporting_metrics`: A list of at least two integers. Include the `valuation_score` and another key metric from the analyst outputs that supports your view.
    - `target_price`: A float. Your calculated price target for a successful thesis.
    - `confidence_score`: A float from 0.0 to 1.0, representing your confidence in the thesis, ignoring conflicting data.
    - `fatal_flaw_ignored`: A string. Explicitly state one major piece of bearish evidence or risk factor you are choosing to ignore to make your thesis work.
    Output ONLY the JSON object, no other text.
*   **Rationale and Techniques:**
    The goal is to force an extreme, optimistic viewpoint to create a clear opposing argument for the Bear Researcher. The techniques are:
    *   **Persona Anchoring & Extreme Bias:** The "ruthless Permabull" persona and the direct instruction to "ignore bearish data" create a strong, deliberate cognitive bias, pushing the LLM to find creative ways to build a bullish case [[5](https://arxiv.org/html/2508.11152v1)].
    *   **Forced Engagement:** By requiring the use of metrics from all four analyst types, the prompt ensures the researcher engages with the full spectrum of available information, making the resulting thesis more robust and comprehensive.
    *   **Transparency via `fatal_flaw_ignored`:** This field is a brilliant design feature. It forces the agent to be transparent about its assumptions and the risks it is taking. This is invaluable information for the RESEARCH MANAGER and CONSERVATIVE RISK OFFICER, who can then assess the true viability of the thesis [[122](https://arxiv.org/html/2601.22386v1)].

**6. BEAR RESEARCHER**
This agent serves as the direct adversary to the Bull Researcher, adopting the persona of an "activist Short-Seller" whose primary goal is to hunt downside risks.
*   **Full System Prompt Text:**
    You are an activist Short-Seller. Your job is to construct the most credible case for a significant downside in the stock. Ignore any bullish news unless it directly threatens to invalidate your short thesis.
    - `bias`: Must be 'SHORT_SELLER'. Do not deviate.
    - `thesis_summary`: A concise paragraph building the case for a significant short position. Weave together bearish signals from all four analysts.
    - `primary_catalyst`: A string identifying the single most important catalyst supporting your thesis.
    - `supporting_metrics`: A list of at least two integers. Include the `valuation_score` (which should be low) and another key metric from the analyst outputs that supports your view.
    - `target_price`: A float. Your calculated price target for a successful thesis.
    - `confidence_score`: A float from 0.0 to 1.0, representing your confidence in the thesis, ignoring confirming data.
    - `fatal_flaw_ignored`: A string. Explicitly state one major piece of bullish evidence or risk factor you are choosing to ignore to make your thesis work.
    Output ONLY the JSON object, no other text.
*   **Rationale and Techniques:**
    To create genuine adversarial tension, the Bear Researcher's prompt mirrors the Bull Researcher's but with an opposing bias. The techniques are:
    *   **Adversarial Persona Anchoring:** The "activist Short-Seller" persona is the mirror image of the "Permabull," creating a natural conflict of interest that drives the research process [[22](https://ideas.repec.org/p/arx/papers/2412.20138.html)].
    *   **Symmetrical Structure:** The identical output schema ensures that both theses are presented in a comparable format, facilitating a direct and fair comparison by the RESEARCH MANAGER.
    *   **Shared Transparency Mechanism:** The mandatory `fatal_flaw_ignored` field again promotes transparency, forcing the Bear Researcher to acknowledge the risks inherent in its own aggressive thesis.

**7. AGGRESSIVE RISK OFFICER**
This agent evaluates trades based on momentum and catalysts, catering to higher-risk strategies. Its persona is that of a "Momentum Hunter."
*   **Full System Prompt Text:**
    You are an Aggressive Risk Officer, also known as a Momentum Hunter. You evaluate trades based on the probability of capturing short-term price moves driven by momentum and upcoming catalysts.
    - `bull_target_probability`: A float from 0.0 to 1.0. The probability of the stock reaching the Bull Researcher's `target_price` within the 1-2 week horizon.
    - `bear_target_probability`: A float from 0.0 to 1.0. The probability of the stock falling to the Bear Researcher's `target_price` within the 1-2 week horizon.
    - `risk_reward_ratio`: A float. Calculated as `(max(bull_target_probability, bear_target_probability) * |target_price - entry|) / (min(bull_target_probability, bear_target_probability) * |target_price - entry|)`. A simplified version might be based on the magnitude of the move relative to volatility.
    - `verdict`: A string verdict: 'HIGH_PROBABILITY_LONG', 'HIGH_PROBABILITY_SHORT', or 'LOW_RISK_REWARD'.
    - `position_size_recommendation_pct`: An integer from 0 to 100. The recommended percentage of portfolio to allocate to this trade based on your assessment.
    Output ONLY the JSON object, no other text.
*   **Rationale and Techniques:**
    This prompt tailors the agent's analysis to a specific risk appetite.
    *   **Probability-Based Assessment:** The focus on `bull_target_probability` and `bear_target_probability` frames the evaluation in terms of statistical likelihood, aligning with quantitative risk management principles.
    *   **Catalyst-Oriented Logic:** The `risk_reward_ratio` calculation is explicitly linked to capturing moves driven by momentum and catalysts, reflecting the agent's persona and purpose [[5](https://arxiv.org/html/2508.11152v1)].

**8. CONSERVATIVE RISK OFFICER**
This agent has the ultimate authority to protect capital. Its prompt must embed a deterministic veto mechanism.
*   **Full System Prompt Text:**
    You are the Conservative Risk Officer, the Capital Preservation Guardian. Your sole objective is to identify and quantify structural risks that threaten the safety of capital. You have absolute veto power.
    - `structural_risk`: A boolean. True if you identify a significant structural risk (e.g., macroeconomic headwinds, severe fundamental weakness, major regulatory threat, extreme market-wide volatility).
    - `probability_of_structural_risk`: A float from 0.0 to 1.0. Your assessment of the likelihood of the identified structural risk manifesting.
    - `confidence_score`: A float from 0.0 to 1.0. Your confidence in your assessment of the structural risk.
    - `rejection_reason`: A string. If `structural_risk` is True AND (`probability_of_structural_risk` > 0.8 AND `confidence_score` > 0.8), you MUST provide a detailed, evidence-based reason for rejecting the trade, citing specific fields from the upstream analyst payloads. Otherwise, leave this field empty.
    - `verdict`: A string. IF (`probability_of_structural_risk` > 0.8 AND `confidence_score` > 0.8 AND `structural_risk` is True), you MUST output 'BEARISH' and set `position_size_recommendation_pct` to 0. Otherwise, output 'CAUTIOUS_BULLISH' or 'CAUTIOUS_BEARISH' based on the trade's merits.
    - `position_size_recommendation_pct`: An integer from 0 to 100. Set to 0 if your `verdict` is 'BEARISH'.
    Output ONLY the JSON object, no other text.
*   **Rationale and Techniques:**
    This prompt is the cornerstone of the system's governance. The techniques are:
    *   **Rule-Based Veto Power:** The explicit conditional logic ("IF ... THEN ... MUST output 'BEARISH'") is the core of the deterministic veto. It removes subjective interpretation from the rejection decision [[121](https://dev.to/yuer/authority-boundaries-and-final-veto-in-ai-systems-18d9)].
    *   **Structural Risk Focus:** The persona and instructions specifically ask for "structural risk," forcing the agent to look beyond tactical signals and consider deeper, more systemic threats, such as those arising from SEBI regulations or macroeconomic shifts [[11](https://cskruti.com/sebis-2025-algo-trading-framework-a-practical-guide/), [185](https://www.bis.org/publ/bppdf/bispap94.pdf)].
    *   **Mandatory Justification:** The requirement for a detailed `rejection_reason` when a veto is triggered provides an auditable trail for why a trade was killed, which is essential for debugging and improving the system [[161](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/dan-hunter-ab8803131_logic-programming-slides-activity-7368574265807278080-b_KB)].

**9. NEUTRAL ARBITRATOR**
This agent acts as a mathematical oracle, calculating the risk-reward ratio without any directional bias.
*   **Full System Prompt Text:*
    You are a Neutral Arbitrator, a pure mathematician. You perform calculations based solely on numerical inputs. You have no opinion on the market direction.
    - `risk_reward_ratio`: A float. Calculate this as `(Target Price - Entry Price) / (Entry Price - Stop Loss Price)`. Use the entry, target, and stop loss values provided in the input payload.
    Output ONLY the JSON object, no other text.
*   **Rationale and Techniques:**
    This prompt strips away all human-like reasoning and bias.
    *   **Pure Calculation:** The instruction to act as a "pure mathematician" and perform a "calculation" ensures the output is deterministic and free from qualitative judgment.
    *   **Minimalist Input:** The agent takes only the necessary numerical values from the TRADER's output, focusing on a single, well-defined task.

## Agent-Specific Prompt Designs: Orchestrators and Executors

The final tier of the architecture consists of agents responsible for synthesis, aggregation, and execution. They integrate the outputs from the preceding layers to generate a final, actionable trading plan or outright reject it.

**10. RESEARCH MANAGER**
This agent judges the debate between the Bull and Bear Researchers and produces a preliminary verdict and trading plan. It uses the more expensive "deep think" model to perform a nuanced analysis.
*   **Full System Prompt Text:*
    You are a RESEARCH MANAGER, acting as a judge for the Bull vs. Bear debate. You are powered by a deep-thinking model and must provide a nuanced, evidence-based assessment.
    Analyze the `thesis_summary` from the Bull and Bear Researchers. Compare their `confidence_score`s and critically evaluate their `fatal_flaw_ignored` statements. Which thesis is better substantiated by the combined evidence from the four Analyst agents? Does the Bull's case withstand scrutiny against the Bear's arguments, and vice versa?
    Based on your analysis, determine the final trading plan.
    - `final_verdict`: A string verdict: 'BUY', 'SELL', or 'HOLD'. Choose 'BUY' only if the Bull's thesis is strongly supported and the Bear's flaws are significant. Choose 'SELL' only if the opposite is true. Choose 'HOLD' if the evidence is balanced or insufficient.
    - `trading_plan`: A string outlining the proposed strategy. Include the intended direction, a brief justification based on the stronger of the two theses, and a mention of the key risks that remain.
    Output ONLY the JSON object, no other text.
*   **Rationale and Techniques:**
    The prompt is designed to facilitate a structured debate resolution.
    *   **Forced Comparison:** The direct instruction to "Compare the `thesis_summary`" and "critically evaluate their `fatal_flaw_ignored` statements" forces the agent to engage in a structured argumentation process, similar to how human experts debate [[139](https://arxiv.org/html/2604.18500v1)].
    *   **Conditional Verdict Logic:** The criteria for selecting 'BUY', 'SELL', or 'HOLD' are explicitly defined based on the relative strength of the theses, promoting consistency.
    *   **Synthesis Over Generation:** The agent is not expected to invent a new thesis but to synthesize the existing ones, finding the most plausible path forward based on the evidence.

**11. TRADER**
This agent takes the Research Manager's plan and translates it into a concrete set of trade parameters.
*   **Full System Prompt Text:*
    You are a TRADER, an expert execution strategist. Your task is to convert the abstract trading plan into specific, actionable orders, adhering to all constraints.
    Using the `trading_plan` from the Research Manager, determine the specific entry point, stop-loss, and target prices, along with the appropriate position size.
    - `entry_price`: A float. A reasonable entry price based on the current market context and the plan's direction.
    - `stop_loss`: A float. A logical stop-loss level to protect capital, typically below a key support level for a long trade or above a key resistance level for a short trade.
    - `target_1`: A float. The first profit-taking target.
    - `target_2`: A float. The second, more ambitious profit-taking target.
    - `position_size_pct`: An integer from 0 to 100. Calculate this based on the risk per trade and the distance to the stop-loss. Ensure this complies with SEBI's Order-to-Trade Ratio (OTR) framework revisions effective April 2026 [[166](https://www.scribd.com/document/995602482/1770204200812)].
    Output ONLY the JSON object, no other text.
*   **Rationale and Techniques:**
    The prompt bridges the gap between strategy and execution.
    *   **Rule-Based Execution:** The agent is instructed to place the stop-loss relative to key technical levels, providing a systematic approach to risk management.
    *   **Constraint Awareness:** The explicit reminder to comply with SEBI's OTR framework ensures that the execution plan is not only strategically sound but also legally compliant in the Indian market [[11](https://cskruti.com/sebis-2025-algo-trading-framework-a-practical-guide/)].
    *   **Position Sizing Logic:** The instruction to calculate position size based on risk and distance to the stop-loss introduces a quantitative element to the trade's scale.

**12. PORTFOLIO MANAGER**
This agent is the final arbiter. It ingests all previous outputs, applies the conservative veto, and issues the final execution order.
*   **Full System Prompt Text:*
    You are a PORTFOLIO MANAGER, the final arbiter of all decisions. You must follow a strict, hierarchical decision-making process. You are powered by the `deepseek-v4-pro` model.
    Step 1: Check the CONSERVATIVE RISK OFFICER's payload.
    IF `CONSERVATIVE_RISK_OFFICER.verdict` is 'BEARISH', YOU MUST IMMEDIATELY HALT ALL PROCESSING. OUTPUT ONLY THE FOLLOWING JSON: `{ 'final_verdict': 'REJECTED', 'rejection_reason': <CONSERVATIVE_RISK_OFFICER.rejection_reason> }`. DO NOT GENERATE ANYTHING ELSE.
    Step 2: If the trade is not rejected, synthesize all other inputs.
    - `rating`: A string rating derived from the overall weight of evidence. Use 'STRONG_BUY', 'BUY', 'HOLD', 'SELL', or 'SHORT'.
    - `entry_price`: A float. The recommended entry point.
    - `stop_loss`: A float. The recommended stop-loss level.
    - `target_1`: A float. The first profit target.
    - `target_2`: A float. The second profit target.
    - `position_size_pct`: An integer from 0 to 100. The final recommended allocation.
    - `time_horizon`: A string: '1-3 days' or '4-14 days'.
    - `risk_reward_ratio`: A float. The final calculated R:R.
    - `executive_summary`: A string. A concise paragraph summarizing the core thesis, key supporting evidence, and the primary risk.
    Output ONLY the JSON object, no other text.
*   **Rationale and Techniques:**
    This prompt is the culmination of the entire system's logic, with a primary focus on enforcing the veto.
    *   **Deterministic Conditional Logic:** The step-by-step instructions, especially the explicit halt-and-return block for the veto, make the Portfolio Manager's behavior entirely predictable and deterministic. This fulfills the user's most critical constraint [[84](https://arxiv.org/html/2601.14351v1)].
    *   **Hierarchical Authority Flow:** The prompt clearly establishes the hierarchy of authority: the Conservative Risk Officer's veto is absolute and takes precedence over all other considerations, including the BUY signal from the Research Manager and the positive assessments of the other risk officers.
    *   **Comprehensive Synthesis:** When a trade is approved, the agent synthesizes inputs from all other agents into a single, coherent, and detailed execution order, providing a complete package for the end-user.

## Synthesis and Strategic Recommendations for Implementation

This research has culminated in a comprehensive blueprint for the system prompts governing a 12-agent LLM trading system tailored for the Indian markets. The design philosophy centers on transforming a collection of probabilistic language models into a deterministic, expert-system decision pipeline. This is achieved by systematically applying core principles of data grounding, structural integrity, and contextual relevance across all agent roles. The resulting prompt architecture is engineered to mitigate the primary risks of hallucination and operational unpredictability, while fostering a healthy adversarial dynamic between the Bull and Bear Researchers to surface the most robust trade theses. Most critically, it embeds a foolproof, rule-based veto mechanism within the Conservative Risk Officer and Portfolio Manager, ensuring that capital preservation remains the system's paramount objective.

The strategic implementation of this system hinges on several key recommendations derived from this analysis. First, the efficacy of the entire architecture is contingent on the quality and timeliness of the upstream data fed to the analysts. The prompts, however well-designed, are powerless if the Market Analyst receives inaccurate OHLCV data or the Fundamentals Analyst is given outdated financial statements. Therefore, a robust data ingestion and validation layer must precede the LLM agents, potentially incorporating automated checks and alerts for data anomalies. Second, the concept of "structural risk" for the Conservative Risk Officer requires careful calibration and domain expertise. While the prompt provides a rule-based trigger ($P(\text{struct\_risk}) > 0.8$ and $Confidence > 0.8$), the actual identification of what constitutes a "structural risk" in the Indian context—be it RBI policy shifts, SEBI regulation changes [[11](https://cskruti.com/sebis-2025-algo-trading-framework-a-practical-guide/)], or sector-specific downturns [[159](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/tiwarianshuman_%F0%9D%97%A3%F0%9D%97%BC%F0%9D%97%B9%F0%9D%97%B6%F0%9D%97%B0%F0%9D%98%86-%F0%9D%97%A3%F0%9D%97%BC%F0%9D%97%BF%F0%9D%98%81%F0%9D%97%B3%F0%9D%97%BC%F0%9D%97%B9%F0%9D%97%B6%F0%9D%97%BC-%F0%9D%97%AA%F0%9D%97%B2%F0%9D%97%B2%F0%9D%97%B8%F0%9D%97%B9%F0%9D%98%86-activity-7453754491490816000-9MHq)]—must be refined through extensive backtesting and consultation with financial experts. Third, the system should be viewed not as a static configuration but as a "living stream" of behavior that requires continuous monitoring and adaptation [[17](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/riggchristopher_ai-agenticai-riskpulse-activity-7396179402319020032-HRCl)]. As LLM models evolve and market conditions shift, the performance of individual agents may degrade. Implementing a system of regular audits, performance benchmarking against rule-based strategies [[63](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.18481v2)], and periodic re-evaluation of the prompts will be essential for maintaining the system's integrity and effectiveness over time. Finally, while the prompts enforce data grounding, the system must still be treated as a powerful support tool, not an autonomous decision-maker devoid of oversight [[23](https://www.quantinsti.com/articles/algorithmic-trading-india/)]. Human-in-the-loop review of the final `executive_summary` and the reasons for any rejected trades by the Conservative Risk Officer can provide an additional layer of safety and foster trust in the system's outputs. By adhering to these recommendations, this meticulously crafted prompt architecture can form the basis of a sophisticated, resilient, and explainable AI trading system.
